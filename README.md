# VidGrab

**Search. Preview. Download.**  
The fastest way to grab videos from the web.

---

### What is VidGrab?

VidGrab is a modern video downloader built for speed and simplicity.  
Paste a link. Choose your quality. Download in seconds.

No accounts. No limits. No nonsense.

---

### Why people use VidGrab

- **Lightning-fast downloads**  
  Get MP4 and MP3 in the quality you actually want — 1080p, 720p, 480p, or pure audio.

- **Clean search experience**  
  Search YouTube and instantly preview titles, channels, duration, and thumbnails.

- **Beautiful neo-brutalist interface**  
  Bold, sharp, and distraction-free. Designed to feel fast and modern.

---

### How it works

1. Paste any supported video link  
2. Pick your preferred format and quality  
3. Hit download  

That’s it.

VidGrab handles the rest behind the scenes — extracting the best available stream and delivering it directly to you.

---

### Built for performance

VidGrab runs on a modern stack:

- React + Vite for a snappy frontend  
- Node.js backend  
- Cobalt-powered media extraction  
- Docker-ready for easy deployment

Everything is optimized to feel instant.
